# Scope and Limitations

The scope of this study is the design and development of CaterHub, a web and mobile application that simplifies the process of catering service procurement. The focus of the platform is put into several main functions: discovering catering services through location-based search, automatic pricing calculation depending on distance and selected services, comprehensive booking management, integrated payment processing, and administrative oversight. The system presents a facility for searching and screening catering providers, choosing menu packages with customization options, finding nearby caterers using real-time geolocation data, and calculating service costs according to distance-based delivery fees and package pricing. 

The platform includes a complete booking system that handles event date selection, guest count management, menu customization with dietary restrictions, and booking status tracking throughout the service lifecycle. Payment processing is integrated through the Xendit payment gateway, supporting multiple payment methods including GCash, PayMaya, and bank transfers, with automatic deposit calculation and real-time payment status updates. For caterers, the system provides business profile management, package creation and editing capabilities, order management tools, earnings tracking through a wallet system, withdrawal request processing, and a premium subscription system for featured listings. Customer features include account management with saved locations, a review and rating system, booking history access, favorites management, and support ticket submission. Administrative capabilities cover user management, caterer application review and approval processes, booking oversight, payment monitoring, refund processing, analytics dashboard with revenue metrics, support ticket management, and system configuration.

The system is developed to simplify the booking process, increase convenience with information access, and deliver a smooth interface for managing catering service requests. It strives to serve both clients and catering service providers by making event planning easier and expanding customer reach with the help of digital transformation.

However, the system is only restricted to a discovery platform, booking management, payment processing, and basic coordination. It does not have inventory tracking, kitchen logistics, or staff management features as these are not part of this version. The application also assumes constant internet connectivity and location services for perfect functioning and may act inappropriately in places with poor GPS signal or mobile data connectivity. The system does not provide real-time GPS-based delivery tracking beyond booking status updates, nor does it include in-app messaging or direct chat functionality between customers and caterers. Integration with external systems such as accounting software, POS systems, or restaurant management systems is not included. The platform relies on third-party services including Xendit for payment processing and OpenStreetMap for geocoding, and may be affected by their availability or rate limitations. The initial release of this application is limited to Android devices and web browsers primarily, with iOS support available but requiring additional optimization. The web version is optimized for desktop and tablet use with limited mobile web optimization. Multi-language support is not implemented, and the application is currently available in English only. The system is designed primarily for the Philippine market, with payment methods and location data focused on Philippine regions, cities, and municipalities. International payment methods or multi-currency support are not available. Advanced security features such as two-factor authentication or biometric login are not included in this version, though standard security measures are implemented. The subscription system is limited to premium featured listings for caterers, and additional monetization features are not implemented. Offline functionality is minimal, and most features require an active internet connection to function properly.
